No new app for your mates. The game moves into the group chat that already exists — here's the flow.

Your crew code

Created in the app (Crew → new). Six characters, like KX4T9C.

2

Add the bot to your group

In WhatsApp, open your group → Add participant → add this number:

+61 493 484 788

WhatsApp has no bot-invite API — this one manual add is by design (your group stays yours; the bot is just a participant).

3

Send the link command

Tap to open WhatsApp with the message ready to send in the group (or DM the bot for solo play):

Open WhatsApp (group) → DM the bot

The message is just link KX4T9C — the bot confirms, and your crew's matches go live in that chat.

4

In person? Scan it

Mates standing around the gym? They scan, tap, linked. The QR encodes the same WhatsApp deep link.

Print it, stick it on the squat rack. (Ask the gym first.)
