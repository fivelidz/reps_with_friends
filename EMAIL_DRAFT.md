**Subject:** Re: Reps With Friends

Ben — good to meet you too. And cheers to Nico for the join-the-dots.

Genuine answer: this is the most thought-through "meaty bones" I've come
across. Two things in particular stood out. The handicap system is the whole
game — it's the difference between a gimmick and something people play for
years, and it's the bit most fitness apps get wrong or don't attempt. And
plugging into the group chats rather than fighting them is the smartest
distribution thinking I've seen in this space. The banter already lives there;
the game should too.

I've already started technical due diligence so our first call is a working
session, not a tour. Three things I've dug into:

- **Rep verification** — it's the hard problem, and I think it's solvable:
  in-browser camera counting (no app install, nothing uploaded) plus
  heart-rate cross-checks from any strap or watch. There's solid open-source
  work we can build on.
- **Chat integration** — I run existing WhatsApp/Signal infrastructure, so a
  working bot prototype is days away, not a discovery project. Slack is
  straightforward too, and honestly the corporate angle there might be the
  quiet business underneath the fun consumer one.
- **Wearables** — mapped the realistic path: phone camera + any BLE heart-rate
  strap first, Apple Watch/Garmin/WHOOP as verification and depth later.

instinct but the money-handling mechanics need a proper legal look before a
dollar moves — that's a solvable structure question, not a blocker. And I'd
park Apple Messages entirely for MVP; no bot API exists, and pretending
otherwise burns budget.

Send the Figma and blueprint across — I'd rather mark them up before we talk.
And yes, genuinely open to the equity conversation; if we're building this
thing properly I'd rather have skin in it than an invoice.

I'm flat out until early next week — does Tuesday or Wednesday suit for a
call? 45 minutes should do it.
