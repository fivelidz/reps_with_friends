## Gotchas hit

- **pkill token must not appear in your invoking command line.** A heredoc
  `bash -c 'cat > /tmp/x.mjs <<EOF …pkill -f TOKEN… EOF; bun /tmp/x.mjs'`
  makes the OUTER bash match the pattern → the tool call dies silently
  (no output, no error). Keep probe tokens out of filenames AND out of the
  bash invocation string.
- **NDC → screenshot mapping must use the canvas rect**, not the viewport:
  the course canvas is only part of the battle screen (quick bar + top bar
  + hand dock own the rest). Mapping NDC to full-shot pixels "finds"
  nothing at the pot/lanes and burns an hour of doubt. Capture
  `canvas.getBoundingClientRect()` in the same probe session as the
  screenshot (drift phase differs across sessions).
- The language sweep now reads `#app` innerText, not `document.body` — the
  founder's own word "TABLE" (camera label) lives in the quick bar; the
  poker/board-word ban still applies to page copy (BANNED regex unchanged).
- Battle/result screens are `calc(100dvh - 44px)` now — if the quick bar
  height ever changes, update BOTH the `.v3-app` padding and that calc.
