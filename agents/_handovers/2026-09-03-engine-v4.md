## The model in one screen

- **Daily battle** (`src/daily.ts`): group config `{targetReps: 200 default,
  playDays, deadlineAt, exercises?, combos?, flags?}`. Everyone races to the
  **RUF** (adjusted) target — handicap multipliers change what a rep is
  WORTH (SOT §3.2), so the same 200 target = different physical targets per
  player (`dailyTargetAdjusted(200, 1.5) = 134` couch, `236` athlete).
  **First eligible player to target = Daily Win; the battle continues** —
  later completers bank the day (completion, streak+1, participation). At
  the (extended) deadline, incompletes fail; an armed **Group Shield**
  converts failures into shielded (streak preserved, shield consumed at that
  close).
- **Power-ups** (`src/powerups.ts`): launch canon — **Lightning** (×3 RUF,
  10 min, 1/day), **Rep Steal = PURE GAIN** (activator gains 10% of the
  target's completed score, target keeps theirs), **Group Shield**
  (streak-protection reading — NOT steal-blocking), **Time Freeze** (+30 min
  group-wide, stack limit flag, default 1). Post-launch set implemented:
  Combo Boost (prescribed sequences via `config.combos`), Double Down (2×
  quest + win-bar flag), Assist Boost, **Surprise Bomb** (+20 RUF in 10 min
  — hit pays a defusal bonus, miss = nothing), **Rescue Rope** (50 credit to
  an *inactive* teammate — counts toward their target), Shield Bash. Our old
  extras (Second Wind, Rabbit's Foot, …) preserved in `POWER_UP_CATALOG`
  with `experimental: true` — activation refuses in v4; mechanics stay in
  the v1–v3 app forks.
- **Seasons** (`src/season.ts`): weekly default (monthly same engine),
  **1 Daily Win = 1 point**, ties recorded not broken (Q224 TODO), streaks
  (+1 bank / preserved shield / reset fail; rest days never break).
  `dayRecordFrom(closedDay, date)` is the glue from day → season.
- **Stakes** (in `src/season.ts`): `{none|dinner|dare|deliverable|charity}`
  objects with declaration locked up front, per-player **agreement gating**
  (nothing active until all accept; decline → void), **resolution at season
  end** (winner = most wins; loser(s) = fewest, bottom tie = joint),
  fulfilment states + evidence. **Charity**: contributions in POINTS (trial
  currency — money later per SOT), winner directs (`designateCharity` —
  only the resolution winner), disclosed `platformFeeRate` (0 in trial).
- **Teams** (`src/teams.ts`): scaffold — min 2/side, uneven (3v2) allowed,
  personal targets kept, scoring PLUGGABLE: `pooled` (Σ RUF vs target×size)
  and `average` (mean completion; default) implemented, `quota` reserved and
  throws (Q229–231 open).
- **RUF** (`src/ruf.ts`): the interim ruling is documented in code — RUF
  internal, "reps" player-facing (`rufToDisplay`), default 200.
