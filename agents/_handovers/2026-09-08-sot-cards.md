**The deal** — day open deals everyone 3 face-down (house auto-picks);
crossing 50% earns a bonus deal (only the player who crossed); hand cap 3;
reroll pays 50/100/200 points to the day pot (players start each day at
500 trial points, ledger on the day). Catch-up curve ported from v1:
behind players draft better rarities (seeded-statistical test proves it).

**The proof flow** — verified logs pay the TARGET +15 (honest incentive);
unverified go to group review in the FEED (accept/contest buttons, live
counts, settles on majority or all-voted; ties accept). Contested sets
score 0 but never un-bank a completed day — never shame, per SOT §2.11.

## Gotchas learned the hard way

- **The deal is RNG** — e2e determinism comes from `RWFSoT.debugGrant(gid,
  memberId, kind)` (same engine grant the app uses), then play through the
  real UI. Never click a card you didn't grant.
- **Overlays compete**: a halfway deal fires the moment a log crosses 50%;
  win moments must be able to sit on top (detectMoments `claimable()`),
  and the deal returns after. Dismiss with "Later"; the waiting chip
  reopens it.
- **Duplicate-warning confirm path** re-submits the set — it must carry the
  `verified` flag (fixed; there's a test + e2e proof of the interaction).
- **Halfway deals only the player who crossed** — dealDay takes `onlyId`.
  Dealing everyone on another player's milestone was a real bug.
- Draft-pick hand cap is enforced in `draftPick`/`draftOptions`; the raw
  `grantPowerUp` (and `debugGrant`) bypass it on purpose (driver).
- Proof +15 lands as an EARNED pseudo-entry (`exerciseId: "prove_it"`) —
  when walking entries backwards for "the last real log", skip powerUps
  entries (the bridge does this).
