Where the build stands

Phase 0 (due diligence) complete. Prototype feature-complete and live — and since 2 Sep 2026, re-spec'd. Ben's master Source of Truth arrived and was reconciled (docs/27): Engine V4 shipped (daily-200 first-to-target battles, weekly 1:1 seasons, all four stakes, corrected power-up canon — 110 tests), the v4 app walks the whole product (120 + 59 e2e checks, zero console errors), and the critical states exist as real states. The stack underneath is unchanged: a tested game engine, two chat bots, a phone-first app, an ops console, an AI layer, push-to-deploy hosting. Remaining for MVP proper: the SOT bot port (in flight), state unification (T5), auth (T4), first real crews, and the pilot gates (WhatsApp verification, Stripe pots, legal opinion).
