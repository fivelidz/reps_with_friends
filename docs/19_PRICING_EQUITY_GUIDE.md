# Pricing & Equity Guide — Reps With Friends

*For Alexei. How to price this project and structure the equity conversation
with Ben. Written 29 Aug 2026, after the working system exists — that fact
changes everything about this negotiation.*

---

## 1. Where you actually stand

Negotiate from the position the demo creates, not from the position the email
implied. Three weeks ago this was "Ben has an idea and a Figma, wants a
builder." Today:

- **The product exists and works.** Engine (98 tests), WhatsApp + Slack bots,
  a phone-first app with camera verification, heart-rate scoring, seasons,
  charity pots — and now Ben's own design running as a complete interactive
  system with a proven engine port (65 screens, 11/11 parity tests).
- **Your velocity is demonstrated, not claimed.** What a studio would scope
  as a 3-month discovery-to-MVP, you produced in days. That's the single most
  persuasive fact you own.
- **His design is already integrated.** You didn't just take the Figma — you
  extracted it, analysed it against an independent design, built its component
  library, and shipped it as a working app. He asked for "a genuine
  collaborator who takes real ownership, not a pair of hands." You've
  pre-delivered exactly that, for free, as a demonstration.

Ben's assets remain real: the idea refined over 18 months, a genuinely
excellent 65-screen design, the Silverchair publicity lever, his network, and
stated willingness to fund some spend. He is a design-and-vision founder who
needs a build-and-systems founder. That's you.

## 3. The equity question — frameworks, then numbers

### Who brought what (the split logic)

| | Ben | You |
|---|---|---|
| Idea & 18 months of refinement | ✅ | — |
| Full product design (65 screens) | ✅ | — |
| Publicity lever + network | ✅ | — |
| Some cash for spend | ✅ | — |
| The working product | — | ✅ |
| Infrastructure (bots, hosting, messaging) | — | ✅ |
| AI/3D/health-data capability (Sahha) | — | ✅ |
| Demonstrated velocity | — | ✅ |
| Ongoing build capacity | — | ✅ |

This is a two-founder company where each brought the hard half of a different
kind. Splits for that shape, in market practice: **55/45 to 60/40** either
direction depending on cash contributed and who's full-time.

### The three structures

**A. Co-founder (recommended) — you: 35–45%, vesting, full IP consolidation**

- Both vest 4 years, 1-year cliff, no exceptions (protects both of you)
- All IP — his Figma/blueprint, your code — assigned to a new company
- You're CTO/co-founder, product decisions shared, his on brand/design/vision
- Little or no cash to you; he funds running costs (they're small: ~$50/mo
  now, more at pilot)
- Why this shape: his own email asked for exactly this ("owning a smaller
  share of something worth a lot beats owning all of something worth nothing",
  "genuine collaborator… skin in the game"). Offer him the structure he said
  he wanted.

**B. Hybrid — reduced cash + 15–25% equity**

- Day rate $700–900 (a visible ~40% discount) through pilot launch, then
  equity-only
- Equity vests on the same 4/1 schedule
- Suits you if you want cashflow during the build. Costs you roughly half the
  equity of option A — the discount IS the equity purchase price.

**C. Pure vendor — $60–90k phased + 2–5% "sweetener"**

- Cleanest, least aligned. He said he'd rather not; you should agree with him.
- Only if the call reveals he actually wants a contractor. Listen for that.

### The valuation sanity check (for your own head, not the call)

Pre-revenue, pre-company, with a working product and a credible founder pair:
angel-stage valuations for this shape in AU run $500k–$1.5M "paper". Your
delivered work ($93–158k equivalent) + remaining build (~$60–100k) is roughly
the company's entire technical foundation. At a $1M paper valuation, $150k of
contributed work = 15% before any risk premium; at-co-founder risk premium and
ongoing commitment is what justifies the 35–45% ask. If he proposes numbers,
this is the arithmetic to run in your head.

## 4. Negotiation guide

**Sequencing:**
1. **Lead with the collaboration, not the number.** "The last three weeks were
   me reading your design properly by building it." Walk him through the demo.
   Let him feel ownership of what his design became.
2. **Establish the frame: two founders, a company, both with skin.** Get his
   nod on the frame BEFORE discussing percentages — a percentage inside the
   vendor frame (10%) and inside the co-founder frame (40%) are different
   conversations.
3. **Then the number.** Anchor at 40–45% (top of the fair range), settle
   happily at 35%. Below 25% with full-time commitment and this much delivered
   work = underpriced; counter with the hybrid (B) instead of accepting.
4. **Then terms** (below). Terms matter as much as points.

**Non-negotiables (your red lines):**
- 4-year vesting with 1-year cliff, **both** founders — his idea included
- **IP assignment of the Figma/blueprint into the company** (if his design
  stays personally owned, your code shouldn't be transferred either — but
  that's a broken company, so just get consolidation)
- Written down: a 2–3 page founders' agreement before the next big build phase
- No equity that vests without the cliff (an investor will retro-fix it
  painfully later)

**Watch-fors:**
- "Equity in place of SOME of the spend" — that's vendor+bonus language.
  Redirect: "if I'm carrying the technical founder load, the split should
  reflect that, and my cash needs can be small."
- A 51/49 "so he keeps control" — 55/60-40 with vesting is normal; 51/49 with
  no cliff means you're an employee with points. Counter: equal board/veto on
  major decisions regardless of split.
- Valuing his "idea + Figma" above everything — the design is genuinely
  excellent and worth real founder credit. It is not worth 80%.

**If he says no equity at all:** the work prices at §2; pilot completion is
$60–90k phased; you stay friendly and take it or leave it on cashflow merit.

## 5. Terms checklist (the founders' agreement)

- [ ] Equity split + 4y/1y cliff vesting, both founders, double-trigger
      acceleration on acquisition
- [ ] IP: his Figma + blueprint, your code, all assigned to the company
- [ ] Roles: he = CEO/product/design/vision; you = CTO/build/systems
- [ ] Decision rights: day-to-day own lanes; major decisions (raise, sale,
      pivot, spend > $X) joint
- [ ] Cash: he funds running costs + any agreed part-time rates; amounts in
      writing
- [ ] Your existing infrastructure (Qalarc Hub etc.) licensed to the company,
      not transferred — protects your other properties
- [ ] Full-time commitment triggers/expectations for both
- [ ] Leavers: unvested returns to the pool; good-leaver/bad-leaver basics

## 6. For the call (extends docs/09_CALL_PREP)

Order of the equity minute: frame (two founders) → demo as evidence of the
collaboration working → the four structural product questions from the Figma
analysis (fairness model, platform, rhythm, power-ups — decide TOGETHER, it
sets the co-founder tone) → split + terms → agreement to have the founders'
agreement drafted within a fortnight.

One sentence worth saying out loud: *"I've already made my bet — the last
three weeks are it. Now we just make the paperwork match."*
